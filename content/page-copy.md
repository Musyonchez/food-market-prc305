## About

### Our story
Big delivery apps list thousands of restaurants, but you never find out who is actually behind the food. Mapishi Market started with a simple idea: put the cook first. Every kitchen on our platform has a face, a story, a menu and honest prices.

### Our mission
To give small cooks and local kitchens a shop window, and to help people eat food made by people they can know.

### What we believe
- **Local.** We back neighbourhood kitchens.
- **Transparent.** The cook, the menu and the price are always visible.
- **Fresh.** Real food, made with care.

### For cooks
Cooks keep control of their menu and prices. We take a small commission on each order. *(Confirm the model with the client.)*

## Privacy and Terms (key points)
- Cooks are independent and responsible for food safety and hygiene.
- Prices are set by cooks. Delivery fees are shown at checkout.
- Orders can be cancelled before the cook starts preparing them.
- We store only the details needed to process orders and don't sell personal data.
