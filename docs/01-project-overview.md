# 01 - Project Overview

## The client's idea (translated)

> "Niliuwa na fikiria naeza tengeneza ya [Food market] kuuza different cuisines kama glovo lakini inaonesha mwenye anapika, menu yake na prices."

Meaning: *"I was thinking I could build a [Food market] that sells different cuisines like Glovo, but it shows who is cooking, their menu and their prices."*

## What we are building

An online **food marketplace** where:

- Many independent cooks / kitchens / small restaurants each get their **own profile page** (photo, bio, location, cuisine, hours).
- Each cook publishes their **own menu with prices**.
- Customers **browse by cuisine**, see *who* is cooking, compare menus and prices, and place an order (or send an order enquiry).

### How it differs from Glovo

| Glovo | This project |
|---|---|
| Restaurant is a listing among thousands | **The cook is the star** - face, story, kitchen |
| Menu buried inside app | Menu and prices are front and centre on the cook's page |
| Needs delivery fleet + app | Website only; delivery/pickup is arranged by the cook (phase 1) |

The **unique selling point** is: *"Know who is cooking your food."* Use this in the presentation and the About page.

## Two goals, one project

1. **Academic:** PRC 305 Web Design & CMS project (Daystar University, 50% of the course grade). See `02-requirements-and-scope.md`.
2. **Client:** A real person commissioned this. The site should be believable and useful enough to hand over.

Hosting is fixed by the university: **InfinityFree** (free PHP/MySQL hosting). This limits some choices, which is why WordPress + WooCommerce + a vendor plugin is the recommended route.

## Working name

Pick a name with the client early. Placeholders: **Mapishi Market**, **Jikoni Hub**, **Kitchen Stories KE**, **Mama's Table**. Check the name is free as a subdomain on InfinityFree (e.g. `mapishi.free.nf`).
