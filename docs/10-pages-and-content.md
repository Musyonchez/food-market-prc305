# 10 - Pages and Content

Page-by-page build spec. Create each under **Pages -> Add New**.

## Home
- **Hero:** headline "Know who's cooking your food." Sub-line: "Discover home cooks and local kitchens near you. See the menu. See the price. Order." Search bar + CTA **Browse Cooks**.
- **Cuisine tiles:** 6-8 categories with images (links to category pages).
- **Featured cooks:** 3-4 cards (photo, kitchen name, cuisine, area).
- **Popular dishes:** product grid (Woo block, "Best selling" or hand-picked).
- **How it works:** 1) Pick a cook 2) Choose your meal 3) Enjoy.
- **Become a Cook** banner with a button.
- **Social proof:** 2-3 short testimonials (from the demo customers).
- **Footer:** links, contact, social icons.

## Browse Cooks
- Dokan store list: `[dokan-stores]`.
- Filters: cuisine/location, search, sort by rating.
- Card: photo, kitchen name, area, rating, "View menu".

## Browse Dishes (Shop)
- WooCommerce shop page with category/price/tag filters.
- Card: photo, dish name, **price**, **cook's name**, Add to cart.

## Cook profile (generated by Dokan)
See doc 08.

## Cart / Checkout / My Account
WooCommerce defaults. Style to match the brand. Change button text to "Place order". Add a note field: "Delivery instructions".

## About
- **Our story:** the client's idea, why it exists, and the problem with impersonal food apps.
- **Mission:** give small cooks a shop window.
- **Team/Founder:** client photo and bio (get permission).
- **Values:** local, transparent, fresh.
- Short **how we make money** note (commission).

## Contact
- **Form fields:** Name (required), Email (required, validated), Phone (optional), Subject (select: Order help / Become a cook / Partnership / Other), Message (required, min length).
- Validation: required fields, email format, spam protection (honeypot or reCAPTCHA), success message.
- Beside the form: email, phone/WhatsApp link, area served, opening hours, an embedded **Google Map**.

## Gallery (optional)
Grid of food photos with a lightbox, filtered by cuisine if possible.

## Blog (optional)
Seed 3 posts:
1. "Cook of the Week: [Name]"
2. "5 dishes you must try in [Area]"
3. A recipe with photos.
Use categories and a featured image on each.

## Legal pages
- **Privacy Policy** (WordPress has a template) and **Terms** (cooks are independent, food safety responsibility, refunds).
- Add a food-safety note: cooks should keep to local hygiene rules.

## Social media integration (minimum 2 platforms)
- Footer icons: Instagram, Facebook (plus X, YouTube or TikTok if the client has them).
- Embed: an Instagram feed on the Home page or a YouTube video on About.
- "Share this dish" buttons on product pages (optional).
- Create the actual accounts first, even if empty, so the links are real.

## Content checklist from the client

- [ ] Final name and tagline
- [ ] Logo (or approval to design one)
- [ ] 4-6 cooks: name, photo, bio, area, phone
- [ ] 4-8 dishes per cook: photo, name, price, description
- [ ] About-page story and founder photo
- [ ] Contact email, phone, social handles
- [ ] Delivery rules (fee, areas, pickup)
