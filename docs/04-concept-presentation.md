# 04 - Concept Presentation (5-7 slides)

Worth **25%** of the grade. The brief requires these topics: domain/background, objectives and justification, executive summary/company profile, target audience and UX considerations, sitemap and wireframes.

## Slide plan

### Slide 1 - Title
Project name, tagline ("Know who's cooking"), your name, PRC 305, Daystar University, date.

### Slide 2 - Domain and background
- Food delivery in Nairobi is booming (Glovo, Uber Eats, Jumia Food), but listings are **restaurant-centric and impersonal**.
- Many home cooks, small kitchens and street-food entrepreneurs are invisible online.
- Client's idea, in their words (quote the original Swahili line, then translate).

### Slide 3 - Problem and objectives
**Problem:** Customers can't easily discover *who* cooks their food or compare menus and prices across small cooks. Small cooks have no affordable storefront.

**Objectives:**
1. Give every cook a profile page, menu and prices.
2. Let customers browse by cuisine and cook.
3. Provide simple ordering.
4. Be responsive and fast on mobile data.

**Justification:** a real client need, a local market, and a clear gap compared with Glovo.

### Slide 4 - Executive summary / company profile
One paragraph: what the marketplace is, who it serves, how it makes money (e.g. 10% commission per order, or featured-cook listing fee). Include the name, logo draft and colour palette.

### Slide 5 - Target audience and UX
| Audience | Needs |
|---|---|
| Hungry students and office workers (18-40, mobile-first) | Fast browse, clear prices, easy checkout |
| Home cooks / small restaurants | Easy way to post menu and prices, no tech skills |
| Event hosts | Find specific cuisines |

UX principles: mobile-first, max 3 taps from home to order, large food photos, visible prices, simple language (English with Swahili touches).

### Slide 6 - Sitemap and wireframes
Show the sitemap from doc 09 and 2-3 low-fidelity wireframes: Home, Cook profile/menu, Cart. Hand-drawn or Figma/Balsamiq screenshots are fine.

### Slide 7 - Tech stack, timeline and next steps
WordPress + WooCommerce + Dokan on InfinityFree. Show the four-week plan from doc 03. End with "Questions?".

## Delivery tips

- 5-7 minutes. One idea per slide, big images, few words.
- Open with the Glovo comparison. It makes the idea clear in 10 seconds.
- Have a likely answer ready for "How will cooks get paid?" (Answer: phase 1 is cash/pay-on-pickup; the M-Pesa plan is in doc 11.)
